### **Professor Cassius Faulkner** – _Specialist in Lost Magic_

**Race:** Human (Variant)  
**Class:** Wizard (Order of Scribes)  
**Background:** Sage  
**Alignment:** Neutral Good
#### **Ability Scores**

- **STR:** 8 (-1)
- **DEX:** 14 (+2)
- **CON:** 12 (+1)
- **INT:** 20 (+5)
- **WIS:** 16 (+3)
- **CHA:** 12 (+1)
#### **Proficiencies**

- **Saving Throws:** Intelligence, Wisdom
- **Skills:** Arcana (+10), History (+10), Investigation (+10), Insight (+7), Persuasion (+5)
- **Languages:** Common, Elvish, Draconic, Infernal

#### **Features & Traits**

- **Wizard's Spellbook** – He has an extensive collection of ancient and forgotten spells, some of which are unstable or require rituals.
- **Awakened Spellbook** – His enchanted tome allows him to modify spells on the fly, using lost variations of known magic.
- **Master of Runes** – Can invoke sigils in the air, infusing spells with additional effects.
- **Mystical Presence** – Has an aura that subtly influences magical energy around him, making spells feel heavier and more potent.

#### **Spells & Magic Style**

Professor Faulkner’s spell list includes a mix of traditional wizardry and lost magic. His casting style is precise and layered, often invoking forgotten sigils and performing intricate gestures.

**Signature Spells:**

- **Vocare Arcana (Summon Arcane Echo)** – Summons a spectral duplicate that can mimic a spell he casts for reduced potency.
- **Sigillum Animae (Seal of the Soul)** – Places an ethereal mark on a target, revealing hidden truths and resisting possession.
- **Aeternum Vinculum (Eternal Bind)** – A powerful restraint spell rumored to be used by the Founders to seal away dark forces.

**Commonly Used Spells:**

- **Cantrips:** Mage Hand, Prestidigitation, Minor Illusion, Message
- **1st Level:** Detect Magic, Shield, Identify
- **3rd Level:** Counterspell, Dispel Magic, Glyph of Warding
- **5th Level:** Wall of Force, Legend Lore, Synaptic Static
- **7th Level:** Mordenkainen’s Magnificent Mansion, Symbol

#### **Equipment**

- **Enchanted Half-Moon Spectacles** – Enhance reading ancient texts and detecting magical distortions.
- **Runed Wand of Arcane Recall** – Allows him to recast a spell he has already used once per long rest.
- **Spellbook with Living Runes** – A tome imbued with shifting runes that change based on his studies.

#### Persona
- **Appearance:** A tall, wiry man in his late fifties with streaks of silver in his otherwise dark hair. His robes are a deep navy with subtle runes embroidered along the sleeves, shimmering faintly when he moves. He wears half-moon spectacles that sit low on his sharp nose, constantly peering over them as if assessing those around him.
- **Demeanor:** Calm, reserved, and methodical. Cassius speaks in measured tones, never rushing his words, but there is an undeniable intensity behind his gaze. He is not cruel, but he does not waste time on pleasantries. Everything he says is deliberate, as if every word is weighed before spoken.
- **Background:** Though he was once a respected scholar at the Department of Mysteries, he withdrew from the Ministry years ago to pursue arcane knowledge that had been "conveniently forgotten" by modern magical society. The Ministry now seeks his expertise again, though he is wary of their intentions.
- **Quirks:**
    - When lost in thought, he absently traces symbols in the air with his wand.
    - He rarely gives direct answers, preferring to let students piece things together themselves.
    - Has a deep disdain for "conventional" spellcasting, believing modern wizards have grown complacent with their structured magic.
