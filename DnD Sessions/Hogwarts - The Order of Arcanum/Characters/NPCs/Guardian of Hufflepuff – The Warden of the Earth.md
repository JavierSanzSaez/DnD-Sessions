**Medium Undead, Lawful Good**  
**Armor Class:** 15 (natural armor)  
**Hit Points:** 90 (12d8 + 36)  
**Speed:** 30 ft.

---

**STR:** 16 (+3)  
**DEX:** 12 (+1)  
**CON:** 16 (+3)  
**INT:** 10 (+0)  
**WIS:** 16 (+3)  
**CHA:** 12 (+1)

**Saving Throws:** Str +6, Con +6, Wis +6  
**Damage Resistances:** Bludgeoning, Piercing  
**Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 14  
**Languages:** Common, Infernal

---

**Traits:**

- **Tough as Earth:** The Guardian has resistance to all non-magical damage.
- **Legendary Resistance (1/Day):** If the Guardian fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.

---

**Actions:**

- **Multiattack:** The Guardian makes two attacks: one with its stone maul and one with its earth strike.
- **Stone Maul (Melee Weapon Attack):** +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.
    - **Hit:** 14 (2d6 + 3) bludgeoning damage.
- **Earth Strike (Melee Weapon Attack):** +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.
    - **Hit:** 11 (1d10 + 3) bludgeoning damage, and the target must make a DC 14 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone.
- **Stone Form (Recharge 5–6):** The Guardian temporarily transforms into stone, gaining immunity to all damage for 1 round.

---

**Legendary Actions (2/Turn):**

- **Stone Maul Strike:** The Guardian can make a melee attack with its stone maul.
- **Earthquake Stomp:** The Guardian slams its foot into the ground, causing the earth to tremble. All creatures within 10 feet of the Guardian must make a DC 14 Dexterity saving throw or take 8 (2d6) bludgeoning damage and fall prone.
