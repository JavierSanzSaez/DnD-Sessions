**Medium Undead, Lawful Neutral**  
**Armor Class:** 14 (natural armor)  
**Hit Points:** 85 (13d8 + 26)  
**Speed:** 30 ft.

---

**STR:** 12 (+1)  
**DEX:** 14 (+2)  
**CON:** 14 (+2)  
**INT:** 18 (+4)  
**WIS:** 12 (+1)  
**CHA:** 14 (+2)

**Saving Throws:** Int +7, Wis +4  
**Damage Resistances:** Psychic  
**Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 11  
**Languages:** Common, Infernal

---

**Traits:**

- **Arcane Focus:** The Guardian has advantage on saving throws against spells and magical effects.
- **Legendary Resistance (1/Day):** If the Guardian fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.

---

**Actions:**

- **Multiattack:** The Guardian makes two attacks: one with its arcane staff and one with its magical bolt.
- **Arcane Staff (Melee Weapon Attack):** +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.
    - **Hit:** 10 (1d8 + 4) force damage.
- **Magical Bolt (Ranged Spell Attack):** +6 to hit, range 60 ft., one target.
    - **Hit:** 14 (2d8 + 4) force damage.
- **Counterspell (1/Day):** The Guardian can use its reaction to cast **Counterspell** when it detects a spell being cast within 60 feet of it.
- **Arcane Burst (Recharge 5–6):** The Guardian releases a burst of magical energy in a 15-foot cone. Each creature in the area must make a DC 14 Dexterity saving throw, taking 18 (4d8) force damage on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one.

---

**Legendary Actions (2/Turn):**

- **Arcane Bolt:** The Guardian can make a ranged attack with its magical bolt.
- **Teleport:** The Guardian magically teleports up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space it can see
