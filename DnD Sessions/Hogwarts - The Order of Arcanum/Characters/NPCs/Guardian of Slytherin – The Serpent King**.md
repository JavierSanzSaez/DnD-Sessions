
**Medium Undead, Lawful Evil**  
**Armor Class:** 16 (natural armor)  
**Hit Points:** 90 (12d8 + 36)  
**Speed:** 40 ft. (climb 30 ft.)

**STR:** 14 (+2)  
**DEX:** 16 (+3)  
**CON:** 16 (+3)  
**INT:** 14 (+2)  
**WIS:** 14 (+2)  
**CHA:** 14 (+2)

**Saving Throws:** Dex +6, Con +6, Wis +5  
**Damage Resistances:** Poison  
**Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 12  
**Languages:** Common, Infernal

---

**Traits:**

- **Serpent's Reflexes:** The Guardian of Slytherin has advantage on Dexterity saving throws against effects that target only the Guardian.
- **Legendary Resistance (1/Day):** If the Guardian fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.

---

**Actions:**

- **Multiattack:** The Guardian makes two attacks: one with its venomous bite and one with its serpent strike.
- **Venomous Bite (Melee Weapon Attack):** +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.
    - **Hit:** 9 (1d6 + 3) piercing damage, and the target must make a DC 13 Constitution saving throw or take 9 (2d8) poison damage.
- **Serpent's Strike (Melee Weapon Attack):** +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.
    - **Hit:** 8 (1d6 + 3) slashing damage.
- **Venomous Aura (Recharge 5–6):** The Guardian exudes a poisonous aura. Creatures within 10 feet of the Guardian must make a DC 14 Constitution saving throw or take 7 (2d6) poison damage.

---

**Legendary Actions (2/Turn):**

- **Serpent's Strike:** The Guardian can make a melee attack with its serpent strike.
- **Cloak of Shadows:** The Guardian magically teleports up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space it can see.
