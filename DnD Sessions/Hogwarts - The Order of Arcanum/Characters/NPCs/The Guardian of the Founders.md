A towering **golem-like statue**, sculpted from **obsidian and enchanted stone**, rises from the far end of the chamber. It features the **four house animals**:

- **The Lion (Gryffindor) – Faces North**
- **The Serpent (Slytherin) – Faces East**
- **The Badger (Hufflepuff) – Faces South**
- **The Raven (Ravenclaw) – Faces West**

Each **animal-head** takes turns channeling magic, their **stone eyes glowing** with energy as they prepare to attack.

#### **Combat Mechanics:**

- The **Guardian takes one action per turn**, and only the active **animal** can be targeted.
- **The animals attack in a fixed order** (Lion → Serpent → Badger → Raven).
- The **inactive animals are immune** to damage as they appear **lifeless stone** during their off-turns.
- The **Guardian’s body itself is impervious**—players must time their attacks **against the active head** before it turns to stone again.

### **Battle Strategy & Weaknesses**

- **Only one animal is active per round**, forcing the party to adapt to changing attacks.
- **Once an animal attacks, it becomes immune while another awakens.**
- **The Guardian’s true weak point lies at its core**, which is only revealed when **all four creatures have less than 20 Hit Points—only then can the final blow be struck.
