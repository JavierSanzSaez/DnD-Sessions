**Medium Undead, Lawful Neutral**  

**Armor Class:** 15 (natural armor)  
**Hit Points:** 80 (11d8 + 24)  
**Speed:** 30 ft.

---
**STR:** 16 (+3)  
**DEX:** 14 (+2)  
**CON:** 14 (+2)  
**INT:** 12 (+1)  
**WIS:** 12 (+1)  
**CHA:** 16 (+3)

---

**Saving Throws:** Str +6, Dex +5, Wis +4  
**Damage Resistances:** Fire  
**Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 11  
**Languages:** Common, Infernal

---
**Traits:**

- **Flame Ward:** The Guardian of Gryffindor radiates an aura of bravery. All creatures within 10 feet of the Guardian have advantage on saving throws against fear.
- **Legendary Resistance (1/Day):** If the Guardian fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.

---

**Actions:**

- **Multiattack:** The Guardian makes two attacks: one with its sword and one with its shield bash.
- **Flaming Sword of Valor (Melee Weapon Attack):** +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.
    - **Hit:** 11 (1d8 + 3) fire damage.
- **Battle Cry (Recharge 5–6):** The Guardian can let out a fearsome cry, forcing all creatures within 30 feet to make a DC 14 Wisdom saving throw or become frightened for 1 minute. A frightened creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.
- **Shield of Honor (1/Day):** The Guardian raises its shield in defense. Until the start of its next turn, it grants itself resistance to all damage and its AC increases by +2.

---

**Legendary Actions (2/Turn):**

- **Flame Strike:** The Guardian can make a melee attack with its flaming sword.
- **Infernal Dash:** The Guardian moves up to its speed without provoking opportunity attacks.
