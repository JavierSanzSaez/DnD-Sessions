### **Core Information**

- **School of Magic:** **Beauxbatons Academy of Magic**
- **Hogwarts House Alignment:** **Ravenclaw** (if she had attended Hogwarts)
- **Background:** _Arcane Archivist_ – A brilliant researcher obsessed with uncovering lost magical knowledge. She spent years in France’s magical libraries, hunting forgotten spells. While her talent is undeniable, her curiosity often leads her into danger.
- **How She Got the Missive:** Celeste was working on translating a long-forgotten grimoire when she accidentally activated a hidden incantation. The spell nearly tore a hole in the fabric of reality, forcing Ministry officials to intervene. Instead of punishment, they offered her a place in the **Order of Arcanum**, believing her expertise would be crucial.
