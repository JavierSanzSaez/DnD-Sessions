_"Strength is found in the earth beneath us, and in those who stand beside us."_
#### **DnD Stats:**

- **STR 14**
- **DEX 10**
- **CON 16**
- **INT 12** 
- **WIS 14**
- **CHA 10**
- **HP:** 36 
- **AC:** 13 (Reinforced Leather Coat)

#### **School of Magic:**

**Ilvermorny – Specializing in Elemental Transmutation & Defensive Warding**

#### **Hogwarts House Alignment:**

Hufflepuff (Had they studied at Hogwarts)

#### **Background:**

Rowan grew up on a **small homestead in the Appalachian Mountains**, far from the grand magical academies of Europe. Their parents, non-magical but deeply connected to the land, raised them to respect nature and the balance of life. Magic did not manifest in them until **their twelfth year**, when an avalanche threatened their village, and Rowan, in a moment of raw instinct, **raised a stone barrier to shield their home**.

At **Ilvermorny**, Rowan showed a natural affinity for **earth-based transmutation and protective magics**. They excelled at reinforcing structures, shaping terrain, and **channeling magic through raw physicality rather than delicate incantations**. Uninterested in politics or prestige, they returned home after their education, using their abilities to **help their community thrive**—mending homes, fortifying farmland, and keeping their people safe.

#### **How They Got the Missive:**

Rowan had never sought out adventure, but it found them when **a strange blight began to spread across the land**—a sickness in the soil itself. **No amount of magic could halt its advance**, and even the most ancient remedies failed. Just as they were preparing to journey in search of answers, a **missive arrived from the Order of Arcanum**, hinting that forgotten magic **might hold the key**. Though leaving home was difficult, Rowan knew their people were depending on them.

## **Core Spells**

#### **🟥 Petrivinculum** _(Earthen Binding – Force Damage)_

_"Petrivinculum!"_

- Rowan **stomps the ground**, causing tendrils of **petrified roots** to **erupt and bind** a target.
- **Effect:** The target must succeed on a **DC 14 Strength Saving Throw** or be **Restrained** for up to 1 minute. They can retry the save at the end of each turn.
- **Damage:** 2d6 **Force**
#### **🟥 Terrafractus** _(Earthshatter – Force Damage)_

_"Terrafractus!"_

- Slamming their fist (or foot) into the ground, Rowan **fractures the earth** in a **15-foot line**, sending a shockwave forward.
- **Effect:** All creatures in the area must make a **DC 14 Dexterity Saving Throw** or be **knocked prone**.
- **Damage:** 3d6 **Force**

#### **🟥 Aggerem Aeternum** _(Enduring Bulwark – Force Damage & Defense)_

_"Aggerem Aeternum!"_

- Rowan raises a **fortified stone barrier** in front of them, shielding allies.
- **Effect:** Grants **half cover** to creatures behind it and absorbs **15 points of damage** before crumbling.
- If cast **directly in front of an enemy**, the impact deals **2d8 Force**

## **Items & Equipment**

#### **🔸 Stonering Bracers** _(Worn on forearms)_

- **Benefit:** Allows Rowan to **channel spells through their fists**, using them as a spellcasting focus.
- **Effect:** Once per short rest, Rowan can **amplify** an earth-based spell, increasing its damage by 1d6.

#### **🔸 Runestone of the Hearth** _(Small carved stone, warm to the touch)_

- **Benefit:** A protective charm that, when activated, **absorbs up to 10 points of elemental damage (Fire, Cold, Lightning, or Acid)** before shattering.

#### **🔸 Reinforced Leather Coat** _(Sturdy, earth-toned cloak with reinforced seams)_

- **AC:** 13
- **Effect:** Provides resistance to **bludgeoning damage** when Rowan is standing on solid ground.
