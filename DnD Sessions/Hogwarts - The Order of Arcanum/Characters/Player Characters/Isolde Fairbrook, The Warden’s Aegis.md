### **Core Information**

#### **School of Magic:**
- **Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry** – Specializing in practical, protective magic that keeps everyday dangers at bay.
- **Hogwarts House Alignment:** Hufflepuff 
#### **Background:** 

Isolde grew up in a modest wizarding household where practicality was prized over flamboyant displays of magic. Her parents ran a small apothecary in a quiet village, and from an early age, Isolde was more interested in spells that mended broken windows or secured the door against stray pixies than in dueling or dark arts. At Hogwarts, she was a steady Hufflepuff who focused on Charms and Defense Against the Dark Arts, always seeking to create reliable, everyday protections. 

After graduation, she worked in the local wizarding community as a ward-maker and caretaker, ensuring that homes and public buildings were safeguarded against minor magical mishaps. A modest but significant incident—a protective charm failing during a routine inspection—sparked her determination to perfect defensive spells. When the Order of Arcanum began seeking out individuals with a practical approach to ancient magic, Isolde was invited to join, driven by her desire to bring robust protections to all who needed them.

#### **How She Got the Missive:**

 A long-time colleague from her work as a local ward specialist—someone who had quietly observed her methodical improvements in protective magic—sent her a discreet note. The missive spoke of forgotten defensive spells hidden within the Hall of Forgotten Arts and hinted that such knowledge could strengthen everyday magical protections, making it a perfect match for her practical expertise. Intrigued and determined to prevent further mishaps in her community, Isolde accepted the invitation.

## **Core Spells**

#### **🛡️ Aegis Fortis** _(Bastion Ward – Protective Spell)_

_"Aegis, protege nos!"_

- Isolde conjures a shimmering barrier around herself or an ally, absorbing **up to 15 points of damage** before dissipating.
- If the barrier is shattered by an attack, it explodes outward, forcing the attacker to make a **DC 14 Dexterity saving throw** or take **2d4 Force Damage**.

#### **🛡️ Contineo Nexum** _(Locking Bind – Utility Spell)_

_"Contineo nexum!"_

- This spell creates a subtle, golden tether between two objects or individuals, preventing forced separation. It can be used to **seal a door or keep an enemy from fleeing**.

#### **🛡️ Reverberatio** _(Spell Rebound – Defensive Countermeasure)_

_"Reverberatio!"_

- When targeted by a spell, Isolde can attempt to deflect it back at the caster, using her deep understanding of magical flows. This spell forces a **spellcaster to re-roll their attack**, potentially turning their power against them.

#### **🛡️ Terra Custodia** _(Guardian’s Ground – Area Defense)_

_"Terra custodia!"_

- Isolde fortifies the ground around her, making it difficult for foes to forcefully move or teleport within a **20-foot radius**. This spell creates an aura that **reduces forced movement by 50%** and **negates attempts at teleportation** within its area.

#### **🛡️ Sanctum Sigil** _(Ward of Resilience – Purification Spell)_

_"Sanctum sigil!"_

- By inscribing a temporary sigil in mid-air, Isolde creates an area of calm that grants **resistance to magical interference** and slowly **mends minor injuries** (restoring **1d4 HP per round** for up to 3 rounds).
