## **Basic Information**

- **Level:** **12** _(A seasoned protector, hardened by years of steady vigilance.)_
- **School of Magic:** **Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry**
- **Hogwarts House:** **Hufflepuff**
- **Background:**  
    Born to a modest, working-class family in the countryside of England, Isolde’s early life was filled with the simple magic of hearth and harvest. Her parents ran a small inn, known more for its warm welcome than its wealth. Though her magical talents emerged quietly, it was her **unyielding sense of duty** that truly set her apart.
    
    She never sought fame or glory; instead, she naturally gravitated towards **defensive magic**, focusing on protecting others rather than seeking power for herself.
    
    After graduating Hogwarts, she worked as a **Magical Wardsmith**, helping towns and wizard communities ward their homes against magical threats. She earned quiet respect from those she helped but remained largely unknown to the wider wizarding world.
    
- **Missive:**  
	A long-time colleague from her work as a local ward specialist—someone who had quietly observed her methodical improvements in protective magic—sent her a discreet note. The missive spoke of forgotten defensive spells hidden within the Hall of Forgotten Arts and hinted that such knowledge could strengthen everyday magical protections, making it a perfect match for her practical expertise. Intrigued and determined to prevent further mishaps in her community, Isolde accepted the invitation.

## **Special Features & Roleplaying Tips**

- **Special Abilities:**
    - _Defensive Mastery:_ Allies within 10 feet gain +1 to their saving throws while Isolde is conscious.
    - _Stonewall Spirit:_ If reduced to 0 HP, she can automatically cast _Protego_ as a last-ditch defense (1/long rest).
        
- **Personality & Roleplaying Notes:**
    - Isolde is **stubborn in defense of others** and **gentle outside of combat**.
    - She **prefers collaboration** over individual glory and will **shield an ally without hesitation**.
    - She tends to **believe in the best** of people, but **trust is not given lightly**.
        
- **Quirks:**
    - Always carries a small stone carved into a badger—_a gift from her younger sister_.
    - Hums old Hufflepuff marching songs quietly when nervous.
    - When preparing for battle, she **whispers the names of people she’s protecting**.
