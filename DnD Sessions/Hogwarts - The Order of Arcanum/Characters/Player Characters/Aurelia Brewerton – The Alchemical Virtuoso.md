## Basic Information

- **School of Magic:** Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry (Potions Specialization)
- **Hogwarts House:** Slytherin
- **Background:**  
    Aurelia Brewerton discovered her innate talent for potion-making early at Hogwarts. Raised in a modest magical household, she spent countless hours in the dungeons perfecting recipes that defied conventional wisdom. Her innovative concoctions earned her a reputation as a prodigy among potion masters.
- **Missive:**  
    While brewing a complex potion one late night in the dungeons, her cauldron began to shimmer with an eerie light. In that moment, an enchanted envelope sealed with dark, ancient wax appeared on her worktable. The note—written in flowing Latin—invited her to join the Order of Arcanum to unlock long-forgotten alchemical secrets.

## Core Spells

1. **Elixir Incendium** (_Incantation: "Elixir Incendium!"_)
    
    - **Type:** Offensive Potion (Fire)
    - **Effect:** Aurelia conjures a volatile vial that bursts forth in a 15-foot cone, dealing **3d6 fire** damage. Creatures caught in the blast must make a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw for half damage.
    - **Thematic Flavor:** The spell mimics the sudden burst of a well-crafted potion, its flames reflecting Aurelia’s fiery passion for alchemy.
2. **Venenum Lenta** (_Incantation: "Venenum Lenta!"_)
    
    - **Type:** Debuff Potion (Venom)
    - **Effect:** Aurelia splashes a thick, noxious brew onto a target within 30 ft. The target must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or have their movement speed halved and suffer disadvantage on Dexterity checks for 1 round.
    - **Thematic Flavor:** A slow, creeping venom that mirrors the careful art of potion-making, disrupting an enemy’s rhythm.
3. **Acidus Caedis** (_Incantation: "Acidus Caedis!"_)
    
    - **Type:** Corrosive Potion (Acid)
    - **Effect:** Aurelia hurls a corrosive potion at a target within 30 ft. The target takes **3d6 acid** damage on a failed DC 15 Dexterity saving throw, or half as much on a success. Additionally, on a failed save, the target’s armor is weakened (AC reduced by 1 until the start of Aurelia’s next turn).
    - **Thematic Flavor:** Reflects the unpredictable and destructive nature of a perfectly brewed acid potion.

## Items & Equipment

- **Brewerton's Cauldron**  
    _Description:_ A finely crafted cauldron etched with ancient runes. It glows with a subtle, pulsing light when used in alchemical processes.  
    _Benefit:_ Grants a +1 bonus on all alchemy-related checks and serves as a focus for potion-based spells.
    
- **Alchemist's Kit**  
    _Description:_ A comprehensive set of enchanted tools—vials, stirring rods, and rare herbs—essential for swift potion brewing.  
    _Benefit:_ Reduces potion crafting time by half and provides advantage on checks involving potion creation.
    
- **Spellbook of Elixirs**  
    _Description:_ A personal grimoire filled with Aurelia’s experimental formulas and detailed alchemical notes.  
    _Benefit:_ Grants advantage on Arcana checks related to potions and alchemy, and can be used to modify potion spells on the fly.

## Special Features & Roleplaying Tips

- **Special Abilities:**  
    Expert in potion crafting; once per short rest, Aurelia can amplify a potion-based spell for an additional **1d6 extra damage**.
- **Personality:**  
    Imaginative, meticulous, and slightly reclusive—Aurelia is driven by a passion for uncovering forgotten alchemical secrets.
- **Motivations & Quirks:**
    - Often talks to her potions as if they were old friends.
    - Has a habit of scribbling down new ideas on any available surface, be it parchment or the back of a cupboard door.
