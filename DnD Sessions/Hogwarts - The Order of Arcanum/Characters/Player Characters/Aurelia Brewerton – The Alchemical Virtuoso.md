## **Basic Information**

- **Level:** **11** _(Highly proficient young witch, focused on the intricate art of potion-making.)_
- **School of Magic:** **Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry**
- **Hogwarts House:** **Ravenclaw**
- **Background:**  
    Born into an unremarkable family of apothecaries and herbalists, Aurelia displayed an **uncanny knack for potions** even before her first Hogwarts letter arrived. What others learned by rote, she discovered intuitively — brewing experimental concoctions that both amazed and alarmed her professors.
    
    Not content with conventional recipes, Aurelia **pushed the boundaries** of what potions could do, blurring the lines between alchemy and spellwork. She was never interested in dueling or political ambition; her loyalty lay with her craft and her endless curiosity.
    
- **Missive:**  
    While browsing the dusty back corner of an abandoned potion shop in Knockturn Alley, Aurelia stumbled upon a **sealed vial** tucked between crumbling recipe scrolls. Inside, suspended in shimmering blue liquid, was a thin strip of parchment. Upon uncorking the vial, the liquid **evaporated into glowing runes** that spelled out a riddle:
    
	_"In the Hall where lost brews sleep,  
	lies knowledge no cauldron may keep.  
	Brew bold, seeker, brew wise—  
	and claim what brews beyond mortal eyes."_
	
	Intrigued — and unable to resist a good mystery — Aurelia saw the riddle as a **personal invitation**. She immediately began preparing for the journey, convinced that **the Hall would house forgotten alchemical secrets** waiting for her discovery.

## **Special Features & Roleplaying Tips**

- **Special Abilities:**
    - _Battle Brewer:_ Once per combat, can concoct a special potion granting advantage on an ally’s saving throw.
    - _Unstable Innovation:_ When casting Alchemy spells, on a roll of natural 20, the effect is doubled (healing, damage, or blinding time).
        
- **Personality & Roleplaying Notes:**
    - Bright, energetic, sometimes a little _reckless_ in her enthusiasm.
    - Believes **every problem can be solved with the right potion**—even problems that clearly can't.
    - **Fiercely curious**, sometimes too much for her own safety.
    - Loves experimenting mid-battle, often _yells out the name_ of a new concoction proudly.
        
- **Quirks:**
    - Keeps a miniature cauldron charm around her neck.
    - Sometimes talks to her potions as if they were living things.
    - Collects rare ingredients obsessively—even if it’s dangerous.
