#### **School of Magic**

- **Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry** – Lyra excelled in Charms and Transfiguration but was notorious for pushing spells beyond their intended limits.

#### **Hogwarts House**

- **Gryffindor** – Impulsive, passionate, and utterly unafraid of magical experimentation, Lyra sees spellcraft as a challenge to be conquered rather than a tradition to be followed.

#### **Background**

Raised by two Muggle scientists, Lyra learned early on that boundaries were meant to be tested. When she discovered magic, she treated it as another scientific field—one that begged to be explored. At Hogwarts, her daring spell experiments often resulted in detentions, but they also led to groundbreaking discoveries. She was the kind of student who would throw herself headfirst into spellcrafting, sometimes literally. After graduation, she turned down prestigious positions in the Ministry to pursue forgotten and experimental magic, seeking to revolutionize wizardry as the world knew it.

#### **How She Got the Missive**

Lyra received a crimson wax-sealed envelope, delivered by an unfamiliar owl. The letter inside burned away upon reading, leaving behind only one sentence: _"Magic belongs to those who dare."_ The invitation to the Order of Arcanum was too tantalizing to resist—secrets, lost spells, and a challenge unlike any before? She was in.

#### **Core Spells**

- **Ignis Tempestas** – Lyra conjures a **raging firestorm**, its flames whipping and surging unpredictably. _(Fire, 3d6, 20ft radius, spreads to flammable objects)_
- **Fulgur Potentia** – A **lightning-infused spell** that crackles with energy, surging between enemies like a storm let loose. _(Lightning, 3d6, 30ft range, arcs between targets within 10ft of each other)_
- **Motus Praecipitis** – A **reckless teleportation spell** that lets her vanish and reappear in a blink—sometimes unpredictably. _(Teleport up to 20ft, roll 1d4: on a 1, she overshoots in a random direction by 5ft)_
- **Resonare Arcana** – A spell that allows Lyra to **read and decipher magical properties** with ease. _(Advantage on Arcana checks for magical items and runes, can attune to items in half the time)_

#### **Items & Benefits**

- **Infernal Conduit Bracer** – A finely-etched armguard that **amplifies fire magic**, adding +1d4 damage to her fire spells.
- **Phoenix-Etched Goggles** – A set of enchanted lenses that shield her vision from blinding lights and flashes. _(Advantage on saves against visual impairments)_
- **The Maverick’s Grimoire** – Unlike traditional spellbooks, Lyra’s tome is scrawled with half-finished incantations and battle-worn pages. It allows her to **modify a spell’s properties**, letting her reroll a spell attack once per long rest.
