_"Strength in body, might in heart; let no foe stand before me."_
## Basic Information

- **School of Magic:** Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry
- **Hogwarts House:** Gryffindor
- **Background:**  
    Ronan grew up in a modest, hardworking wizarding family where physical prowess was prized alongside magic. At Hogwarts, he became renowned for his achievements in Quidditch and dueling tournaments. Eschewing long hours in the library, Ronan honed his body and spellcasting in equal measure—relying on a blend of raw physical power and magical augmentation to dominate in close combat.
- **Missive:**  
    His heroic display in a street duel against rogue dark magic caught the attention of the Order of Arcanum. A discreet messenger delivered a signed missive, inviting him to join the Order because his unique talents were needed to face emerging threats.

## Core Spells

1. **Pugno Incendium** (_Incantation: "Pugno Incendium!"_)
    
    - **Type:** Offensive Enhancement (Fire)
    - **Effect:** Ronan channels magical fire into his unarmed strike, delivering an attack that deals **3d6 fire** damage. The target must succeed on a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw to take half damage. On a failure, the target also suffers an extra **1d6 fire** damage at the start of its next turn.
    - **Thematic Flavor:** The spell adds a searing blaze to his mighty punch, as if his very fist were forged in dragonfire.
2. **Vis Fortis** (_Incantation: "Vis Fortis!"_)
    
    - **Type:** Buff (Enhancement)
    - **Effect:** Ronan boosts his physical strength for 1 minute, gaining a **+2 bonus to Strength-based attack rolls and damage**. He also gains advantage on Athletics checks for grappling or lifting heavy objects during this time.
    - **Thematic Flavor:** A surge of raw, elemental might that makes his blows even more devastating.
3. **Celeritas Agilitas** (_Incantation: "Celeritas Agilitas!"_)
    
    - **Type:** Mobility (Self-Buff)
    - **Effect:** Ronan’s speed and reflexes surge, allowing him to dash an additional 10 ft. (totaling 40 ft. movement) for one round, or to gain an extra reaction to reposition when an enemy moves close.
    - **Thematic Flavor:** He moves with the swiftness of a striking lion, dodging incoming attacks and closing the gap with his foes in a heartbeat.

## Items & Equipment

- **Iron Gauntlets**  
    _Description:_ Heavy gauntlets forged from enchanted iron and inscribed with protective runes.  
    _Benefit:_ Grants a **+1 bonus to unarmed attack rolls** and increases unarmed strike damage by **1d4**, synergizing with _Pugno Incendium_.
    
- **Enchanted Warding Vest**  
    _Description:_ A sturdy, magically reinforced vest that fits like a second skin.  
    _Benefit:_ Increases AC by +2 (total AC 15) and provides minor resistance to bludgeoning damage when on solid ground.
    
- **Stamina Baton (Wand)**  
    _Description:_ A short, baton-like wand designed for quick, forceful spellcasting rather than intricate incantations.  
    _Benefit:_ Functions as his primary focus for casting spells, particularly those that augment his physical prowess.

## Special Features & Roleplaying Tips

- **Special Abilities:**
    - Ronan combines his natural strength with magical enhancements to become a formidable melee combatant.
    - His style is aggressive: he often charges into battle and relies on his physicality to overpower opponents.
- **Personality:**
    - Bold, headstrong, and fiercely loyal, Ronan believes that raw physical power and unyielding courage are the keys to victory.
    - He isn’t particularly scholarly, preferring action over theory, and his confidence sometimes borders on recklessness.
- **Motivations & Quirks:**
    - Determined to prove that physical might is as vital as intellectual mastery in the wizarding world.
    - Frequently flexes his muscles before casting a spell, as if preparing his body for the magic to come.
    - Often shouts his catchphrase, **"Feel the heat of my fist!"**, as he charges into combat.
