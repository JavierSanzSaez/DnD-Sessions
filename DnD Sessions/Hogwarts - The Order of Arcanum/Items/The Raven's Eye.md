**Description:**  
A small, intricate orb made of obsidian and sapphire, constantly swirling with dark blue energy. The orb feels like it could be both a source of insight and a weapon of destruction.

**Identifying the Spell:** The players quickly recognize the orb as a conduit for **destructive energy**, likely tied to **evocation** and the manipulation of arcane forces.

**Spell:** **Thunderclap Burst** (1st-level evocation) 
- The wielder of the Raven’s Eye can cast **Thunderclap Burst** once per day without expending a spell slot. 
> The caster summons a burst of thunderous sound from the orb, creating a 10-foot radius <span style="color:#8844bb">thunderclap</span> centered on a point within 60 feet. All creatures within the area must make a **Constitution saving throw (DC 14)** or take **<span style="color:#8844bb">2d6 thunder</span>** damage and be pushed back 10 feet. A successful save reduces the damage by half and negates the push.
