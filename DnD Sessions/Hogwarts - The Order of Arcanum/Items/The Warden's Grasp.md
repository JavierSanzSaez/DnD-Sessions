**Description:**  
A pair of enchanted gauntlets that feel unusually heavy but perfectly balanced. The gloves are inscribed with runes that glow faintly with golden light, symbolizing endurance and tenacity.

**Identifying the Spell:** Upon inspection, the players realize that these gauntlets can be used to channel **force** and **earth-shaking power**, designed to overpower opponents.

**Spell:** **Earthen Surge** (1st-level evocation) 
- The wearer of the Warden’s Grasp can cast **Earthen Surge** once per day without expending a spell slot. 
> The caster channels the power of the earth, causing the ground to tremble. A **15-foot line** of <span style="color:#cc3333">force</span> energy erupts from the caster, dealing **<span style="color:#cc3333">3d6 force</span>** damage to creatures in its path. Creatures in the line must make a **Strength saving throw (DC 13)** or be knocked prone.
