**Description:**  This **twisted black wand** pulses with a dark, almost sentient energy. Small **etchings of snake fangs** are embedded into the wood, **glowing faintly** in the dim chamber. Every so often, the mist condenses into the **spectral outline of a coiling serpent**, before dissipating into nothingness.

**Identifying the Spell:** The players discern that this wand is tied to **necromancy** or **poisonous magic**, harnessing dark energies to deal damage.

**Spell:** **Serpent’s Venom** (1st-level necromancy) 
- The wielder of the Serpent’s Fang can cast **Serpent’s Venom** once per day without expending a spell slot. 
> The caster unleashes a **poisonous bolt** in a line that extends 30 feet. The bolt deals **<span style="color:#44bb00">2d6 poison</span>** damage on a hit, and the target must make a **Constitution saving throw (DC 13)** or be **poisoned** for 1 minute. While poisoned in this way, the creature takes an additional **<span style="color:#44bb00">1d6 poison</span>** damage at the start of each of its turns.
