**Description:**  
A large, round shield with the emblem of a roaring lion at its center. The shield feels heavy but unyielding, as if infused with the courage of those who stood firm in battle.

**Identifying the Spell:** Upon investigation, the players sense the shield’s magic is connected to **Evocation** and **forceful protection**. It holds a defensive yet offensive potential.

**Spell:** **Lion's Roar** (1st-level evocation) 
- The wielder of the Shield of the Brave can cast **Lion's Roar** once per day without expending a spell slot. 
> The spell summons a torrent of flame from the lion's jaws engraved on the shield, creating a 15-foot cone of <span style="color:#ee5500">fire</span> that deals **<span style="color:#ee5500">2d6 fire</span>** damage to creatures within the area of effect. Creatures caught in the blast must make a **Dexterity saving throw (DC 12)** to take half damage.
