### **Puzzle: The Runes of the Founders**

As the party steps onto the lower floor, the ancient runes **ignite with a soft glow**, shifting between different hues. The entire floor is composed of large stone tiles, each etched with a **single rune** tied to one of the Hogwarts Founders.

#### **Puzzle Mechanic:**

- The runes **humm in sequence**, forming a **pattern** that must be deciphered.
- Players can attempt an **Arcana (DC 14) or History (DC 13) Check** to recall ancient wizarding sigils and understand the **meaning of the runes**.
- If players step on the wrong runes, the room **reacts**—a magical backlash **pushes them away**, dealing **<span style="color:#cc3333">1d6 force</span>** damage and resetting the puzzle.
- They must step on the runes in the correct **Founder’s sequence** to activate the full magic of the chamber.

#### **Clue for the Puzzle:**

- The **correct order** corresponds to the **age of the Founders** (Rowena → Salazar → Helga → Godric).
- An **Investigation (DC 12) Check** might reveal faint markings near the correct runes, as if worn by past users.
- A **Revelio spell** highlights the runes, but the correct ones shine **brighter**.

### **🔐 The Puzzle: Unlocking the Door**
