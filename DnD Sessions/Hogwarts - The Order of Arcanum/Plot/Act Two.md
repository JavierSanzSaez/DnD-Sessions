### **Act Two: Into the Depths of the Hall**

With the **raging waterfall** behind them, the players find themselves standing at the entrance to the **Hall of Forgotten Arts**. The **stone door**—etched with ancient runes—begins to **open slowly**, revealing a dimly lit chamber beyond.

#### **Scene Setup: The Octagonal Chamber**

The party steps into the **octagonal chamber** of the **Hall of Forgotten Arts**, a space filled with an **air of deep, ancient magic**. The walls are **lined with towering bookshelves**, their contents shrouded in the dust of ages—**crumbling, forgotten manuscripts** that speak of spells and knowledge lost to time. The faint scent of **aged parchment** fills the air as the **shelves groan quietly** under the weight of the centuries.

In the **center of the room** stands a towering **stone column**, etched with the faces of the **Four Founders of Hogwarts**—their expressions are **etched with immense detail**, yet their eyes seem to **follow the players**, judging them as they enter. The column **spins slowly**, revealing different depictions of the Founders as it rotates.

From the center of the chamber, **four corridors stretch out**—one in each cardinal direction, leading further into the mystery of the Hall. Each corridor is **flanked by magical flames**, flickering brightly in the colours of the four Hogwarts Houses—**scarlet**, **gold**, **green**, and **blue**. The flames seem to **pulse with energy**, flickering and dancing as if alive, casting eerie shadows on the walls.

#### **The Fire Trials**

Each of the four **House-coloured fires** is a **magical barrier**, and only those whose **magic aligns** with the **respective House** will be able to pass through. As the players move closer to the flames, they can feel the pull of each fire, subtly assessing them.

- **The Gryffindor fire** (red and gold) flickers with a strong sense of **bravery** and **valor**.
- **The Slytherin fire** (green and silver) burns with a cold, calculating **ambition** and a fierce sense of **determination**.
- **The Ravenclaw fire** (blue and bronze) shimmers with an aura of **intelligence** and **curiosity**.
- **The Hufflepuff fire** (yellow and black) glows with a warmth of **loyalty**, **kindness**, and **steadfastness**.

The **pedestals at the end** of each corridor hold **ancient artifacts**, each one containing a **single spell**—unique to each House, a forgotten magic tied to the legacy of the Founders. But **before they can cross the fire**, the party will face a challenge that will test their mettle.

---
#### **Encounter: Guardians of the Hall**

Just as the players approach the **magical flames**, they hear a **low rumbling sound** echoing from the chamber. The air grows **heavy** with tension, and the players feel an unnatural chill creeping in. Suddenly, the ground begins to **tremble** beneath their feet, and the **shadows** cast by the flames **shift unnaturally**.

From the **stone walls**, four figures **emerge**—**ethereal, spectral warriors**, each cloaked in the **colors of one of the Four Houses**. Their **haunting forms** are **bound by magic**, their faces obscured by **tattered robes**, and their eyes glow with a ghostly **white light**. They are the **Guardians of the Fires**, ancient protectors bound to test those who seek the secrets of the Hall.

The **Guardians** seem to **read the players' intentions**, sensing whether their hearts are aligned with the correct House. They raise their **weapons**, each one a **magical artifact** tied to the House they represent, ready to strike down those who seek to pass without proving their worth.

A voice—deep, ancient, and authoritative—**booms from the very walls**:

> **"Only those who prove themselves worthy may pass. Show us the magic of your souls, or be turned to dust beneath our claws."**

The **Guardians of the Four Fires** **lunge forward**, and the battle begins.

---

#### **Combat Encounter: The Guardians of the Four Fires**

- **Guardian of Gryffindor** (Red and Gold): A **warrior of courage and valor**, wielding a **flaming sword**. It tests the players’ **bravery** and **strength**. [[Guardian of Gryffindor – The Flame Warden]]

- **Guardian of Slytherin** (Green and Silver): A **serpent-like figure**, with **scales that gleam** with cold magic. It tests the players' **ambition** and **resourcefulness**. [[Guardian of Slytherin – The Serpent King]]

- **Guardian of Ravenclaw** (Blue and Bronze): An **aviary figure**, with **wings of starlight**, testing the players’ **wisdom** and **intellect**. [[Guardian of Ravenclaw – The Arcane Sentinel]]

- **Guardian of Hufflepuff** (Yellow and Black): A **tall, sturdy figure** made of bark and stone, with a heart of **golden light**. It tests the players' **loyalty** and **steadfastness**. [[Guardian of Hufflepuff – The Warden of the Earth]]

The **Guardians’ attacks** are not designed to kill, but to **test the players' virtues** and force them to prove their worth before passing through the flames. The battle becomes a matter of **resilience**—can the party withstand the tests of their **individual qualities** and **overcome the magical forces** that bind the Guardians?

### **🔥 The Awakening of the Founders’ Column**

As the **last Guardian of the Four Fires crumbles**, the chamber falls into a deep, almost expectant **silence**. The magical flames that had once blocked the corridors **remain steady, flickering gently**, no longer barring passage. A sense of **completion** lingers in the air—something has shifted within the ancient magic of this place.

Each corridor, now free of its magical flames, leads to a **small alcove**, illuminated by a **soft, eerie glow** matching the color of its respective House. At the end of each path, a **pedestal of ancient stone** stands, covered in **dust, cobwebs, and faintly glowing runes** that pulse with an otherworldly energy.

Each artifact is not simply placed upon the pedestal—it is **suspended**, held in a delicate yet unbreakable web of ancient magic, waiting to be claimed.

>[!Griffindor's Aegis]- **🛡️ Gryffindor's Aegis** – The Shield of the Lion
>**Storage:** Encased within a **fiery red stasis field**, this shield hovers an inch above the pedestal, its **metal gleaming as if untouched by time**. The engraved lion on its surface appears almost alive, its **jaws slightly open**, as though waiting to **unleash its fury**. The **protective aura** crackles faintly, radiating waves of heat.
>
>**Interaction:** As a player reaches out, the **stasis field flickers**, testing their resolve. Only someone with the **courage to push through** will break the enchantment, allowing the shield to land **heavily** in their grasp.
>
>**🔸 Ability Checks to Extract**
> - **Strength (Athletics) DC 16** → The player forcefully **pulls the shield** from its pedestal.
> - **Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) DC 16** → The player **carefully pries apart** the magical clamps.
> - **Intelligence (Arcana) DC 14** → The player recognizes and **disrupts the binding magic** before pulling the shield.
>
>**❌ Failure Consequences** 
> - **Magical Backlash:** A fiery **shockwave bursts from the shield**, forcing the player to make a **DC 14 Dexterity saving throw**.
> 	- _Fail:_ They take **2d8 fire damage** 🔥 and are **pushed 5 feet back**.
> 	- _Success:_ They only take **half damage** and can retry next turn.

>[!Slytherin’s Fang** ]- **🐍 Slytherin’s Fang** – The Venomous Wand
> **Storage:** Suspended within a **serpentine vortex of green mist**, this **twisted black wand** pulses with a dark, almost sentient energy. Small **etchings of snake fangs** are embedded into the wood, **glowing faintly** in the dim chamber. Every so often, the mist condenses into the **spectral outline of a coiling serpent**, before dissipating into nothingness.
> 
> **Interaction:** When grasped, the mist **lunges forward like a striking viper**, momentarily wrapping around the wielder's wrist before fading. A faint, **whispering hiss** can be heard, though its words remain unintelligible.
> 
> **🔸 Ability Checks to Extract**
> - **Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) DC 16** → The player swiftly **plucks the wand**, avoiding the venomous tendrils around it.
> - **Intelligence (Arcana) DC 14** → The player correctly **disrupts the warding runes** on the pedestal before touching the wand.
> - **Charisma (Deception) DC 15** → The player convinces the artifact that they are **worthy of its power**, causing it to release itself.
> 
> **❌ Failure Consequences**
> - **Venomous Counterattack:** A spectral serpent emerges, striking the player. They must make a **DC 14 Constitution saving throw**.
> 	- _Fail:_ They take **2d6 necrotic damage** 💀 and have **disadvantage on their next Ability Check** as the poison weakens them.
> 	- _Success:_ They take **half damage** and can retry next turn.

>[!Ravenclaw’s Artifact ]- **📿 Ravenclaw’s Artifact – The Raven’s Eye**
 >**Storage:** Suspended in the air, the **small obsidian and sapphire orb** hovers within a **silent vortex of swirling blue energy**, casting erratic shadows across the chamber walls. Its **polished surface shimmers with unnatural clarity**, revealing glimpses of shifting symbols and **indecipherable runes** within its depths. Every so often, a **faint, whispering voice** echoes from the orb—though its words slip away before they can be fully understood.
 >
> **Interaction:** As a player reaches for the Raven’s Eye, the **orb abruptly halts its slow rotation**, fixing its unseen gaze upon them. The moment their fingers make contact, a **pulse of raw knowledge and power surges through their mind**, filling their vision with flickering images—**blue lightning splitting the sky, cryptic diagrams scrawled in ancient ink, the gaze of a great, unseen intelligence**. The storm around the orb **contracts inward**, crackling as it seals itself within the artifact. For a brief moment, the player feels both **infinitely small and impossibly vast**, before the whispers fade into silence, and the Raven’s Eye rests obediently in their grasp.
> 
>**🔸 Ability Checks to Extract**
> - **Intelligence (Investigation) DC 16** → The player carefully **studies the orb’s magical locks** and disengages them.
> - **Wisdom (Perception) DC 15** → The player notices **a precise pattern in the runes**, allowing them to touch the orb at the right moment.
> - **Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) DC 14** → The player quickly **snatches the orb** between pulses of energy.
>
>**❌ Failure Consequences**
>- **Mental Backlash:** The orb’s latent power **flashes**, forcing the player to make a **DC 14 Wisdom saving throw**.
>	- _Fail:_ They take **2d6 psychic damage** 💜 and are **dazed**, giving them **disadvantage on their next attack roll or skill check**.
>	- _Success:_ They take **half damage** and can retry next turn.
> 

>[!Hufflepuff’s Gauntlets**]- **🌿 Hufflepuff’s Gauntlets** – The Warden's Grasp
> **Storage:** A pair of **stone-carved gauntlets** rest upon a pedestal made of **ancient tree roots**, twisted into a natural throne. Unlike the others, these artifacts are not floating, but rather **embedded** within the roots themselves, as if the earth itself refuses to let them go. A faint **golden glow** pulses beneath the stone, as if something beneath the surface is alive.
> 
> **Interaction:** As a player attempts to pull them free, the **roots tighten**, resisting their grip. Only by exerting **strength and perseverance** (or an act of **patience and care**) will the roots **reluctantly loosen**, allowing the gauntlets to be claimed. A brief **rumble** in the ground signals their awakening.
> 
> **🔸 Ability Checks to Extract**
> - **Strength (Athletics) DC 15** → The player **pries the talisman free** from the pedestal’s roots.
> - **Wisdom (Nature) DC 14** → The player communes with the artifact, **convincing it to release itself**.
> - **Charisma (Persuasion) DC 15** → The player speaks to the magic within, **earning its trust**.
>
> **❌ Failure Consequences**
> - **Root Bind:** The talisman retaliates as **roots shoot out** from the pedestal. The player must make a **DC 14 Strength saving throw**.
> 	- _Fail:_ They are **restrained** until they break free with a **DC 14 Strength check** on their turn.
> 	- _Success:_ They avoid the roots and can retry next turn.

The moment the **last artifact** is claimed, a deep, **mechanical groan** echoes through the chamber. The **central column**, adorned with the **carved visages of the Founders**, **begins to rotate**, slow at first, then gaining momentum. Dust **shakes loose** from the ceiling as the entire structure **descends into the floor**, vanishing inch by inch as if it were being lowered by some unseen force.

As the column sinks, the floor **shudders** and **cracks apart** in a perfectly circular formation. From the widening gap, a spiraling **stone staircase** emerges, its steps forming in a smooth, rhythmic sequence, **locking into place one after another**. At its center, an ancient **sigil pulses faintly**, feeding the last remnants of its energy into the newly revealed passage.

A soft but **chilling draft** rises from below, carrying the scent of **dust, parchment, and something older—something untouched for centuries**. The air is heavy, charged with magic that has not been disturbed in an age.

The path forward is clear. The only question is—**what awaits them below?**
