# **The Meetup with Professor Faulkner & The Path to the Hall of Forgotten Arts**

#### **Scene Setup: The Grand Study**

The players arrive at **Hogwarts** as instructed, their summons guiding them to a secluded part of the castle. They are led—whether by a **silent house-elf**, a **moving staircase that seems to direct them**, or even **a whispering portrait**—to an old **grand study** deep within the **west wing of the castle**, a place that was long thought abandoned.

This study is lined with **ancient tomes**, glowing sigils hover in the air, and the walls are covered in **floating runic inscriptions** that shift when observed. The air feels **heavy with magic**, the kind not commonly taught at Hogwarts anymore.

At the center, **Professor Cassius Faulkner** stands before a massive **obsidian table**, scattered with **handwritten notes, enchanted quills, and a rotating three-dimensional model of Hogwarts** with pulsing blue dots marking different locations. His robes seem to **move subtly on their own**, as if woven with living runes.

---
### **How the Conversation Flows**

#### **1. The Players Arrive (First Impressions)**

- The players **do not know each other** yet, so this moment allows for character introductions.
- Cassius acknowledges their arrival but **does not introduce himself outright**—he prefers to let the players **observe and deduce**.
- He studies them for a moment, murmuring something under his breath (perhaps noting magical potential or peculiarities).
- Then, without looking up, he **magically pulls a chair out for each of them** with a flick of his wand, gesturing for them to sit.

#### **2. The Briefing** _(“You are not here by accident.”)_

Once the players have settled, Cassius speaks in a **calm but deliberate tone**:

> _"The Ministry claims that magic is understood. That all spells have been categorized, documented, controlled. They are mistaken."_  
> _"Magic is a living thing. It shifts. It adapts. And most importantly—it forgets."_

Cassius **places his wand on the table**, and the **model of Hogwarts shifts**, revealing a hidden **section deep beneath the castle**—the **Hall of Forgotten Arts**. The blue pulses seem almost alive, flickering erratically.

> _"The Hall of Forgotten Arts... a place of legend, lost to time. But it is real. And for the first time in over a century, it stirs."_

#### **3. The Players’ Role & the Invitation to Enter the Hall**

Cassius tells them that the Hall only **opens once every century** when the **Full Moon crosses a conjunction between specific constellations**. 

The constellations are:
##### **1. Luminara** (The Guiding Star)

- **Description:** Luminara is a constellation shaped like a radiant star, with its rays extending outward in every direction. It represents guidance and illumination, leading those who seek knowledge in times of darkness.

> [!Intelligence Check]- Intelligence Check - DC 10
> **Significance:** It is said that when the Full Moon crosses Luminara’s conjunction, the way to the Hall becomes clear, as though the very stars are aligning to reveal ancient secrets.
##### **2. Pyroscorch** (The Burning Serpent)

- **Description:** A long, serpentine constellation of swirling flame and ember, representing both destruction and rebirth. Pyroscorch is often associated with dangerous magic and the transformation of raw power.

> [!Intelligence Check]- Intelligence Check - DC 10
> **Significance:** When the Full Moon aligns with Pyroscorch, it signals the moment when the dormant power of the Hall begins to stir, preparing for the trials ahead.
##### **3. Astralith** (The Astral Bridge)

- **Description:** A narrow bridge of stars stretching from one side of the sky to the other, symbolizing the connection between realms and dimensions, a passageway between the known and unknown.

> [!Intelligence Check]- Intelligence Check - DC 10
> **Significance:** The conjunction of the Full Moon and Astralith is believed to mark the time when the boundary between the material world and the hidden realms within the Hall of Forgotten Arts is thinnest.
##### **4. Vesperis** (The Twilight Crown)

- **Description:** Vesperis forms a crown of stars encircling the northern sky, representing twilight, the liminal space between night and day, and the mysteries that lie between worlds.

> [!Intelligence Check]- Intelligence Check - DC 10
> **Significance:** The Full Moon crossing Vesperis is the final marker that seals the opening of the Hall. It signifies that the ancient wards are weakening, allowing access to the forgotten magic inside.

This rare alignment is occurring soon, and the Hall will soon become accessible.

- The **Ministry has sanctioned this expedition**, but the risk is theirs alone.
- Only those who prove themselves can **pass through the trials** within.
- The founders themselves are said to have **woven magic into this place**—and it does not welcome intruders lightly.
- Inside, the Hall is rumored to contain a **powerful artifact**, the true nature of which remains unknown. Its power is said to rival any magical relic in existence, and its discovery could alter the balance of magic itself.

At this point, Cassius **looks at each player in turn**, evaluating them. He then says:

> _"You were not chosen for your titles or past accomplishments. You were chosen because magic itself seems to... favor you. And we are running out of time."_

He motions towards a **sealed doorway at the back of the study**, one covered in **serpentine, avian, leonine, and badger-like motifs**—one for each **Hogwarts founder**.

> _"If you accept, the door will open for you. If you hesitate, the Hall will sense your doubt."_

---

### **The Path to the Hall of Forgotten Arts**

#### **1. The Founder’s Door** _(A Symbolic Test)_

The **door** at the back of the study is not just a normal entrance—it is **a test in itself**.

- Each player must **approach and interact** with the door in some way.
- The magic within the door will react differently to each character based on their **attitude, background, and approach to magic**.
- For instance:
    - A **bold** character might feel the lion's engraving **heat up** beneath their touch (Gryffindor).
    - A **cautious, cunning** character might see the serpent’s eyes **glow** as if watching them (Slytherin).
    - A **scholarly** character may hear faint **whispers of forgotten spells** (Ravenclaw).
    - A **nurturing or steadfast** character might feel the door's warmth **pulse like a heartbeat** (Hufflepuff).

**Outcome:**

- If they step forward **without fear**, the door **shifts and unlocks**.
- If they hesitate, the runes flicker **uncertainly**—Cassius simply watches, offering no encouragement.

Once they all pass through, the stone passage **spirals downward** into the hidden depths of Hogwarts. The walls are lined with **ancient murals depicting forgotten spells**, some of which **seem to react to their presence**.

---

### **The First Puzzle: The Stone Bridge & The Dark Waterfall**

#### **Scene Setup: The Bridge of the Hall**

The players step onto a **vast stone bridge**, seemingly suspended in mid-air, stretching over an abyss of **blackness**. The bridge is ancient, weathered by time, but still solid, its stones cold and smooth to the touch. The atmosphere here is thick with the weight of centuries, and a chill runs through the air as though the bridge is a forgotten path leading into unknown depths.

At the **center of the bridge**, a strange phenomenon occurs. A **pitch-black waterfall** begins to form directly above them, cascading down from the ceiling with an eerie **silent roar**. The water flows in a thick, viscous manner, like liquid shadow, its surface reflecting no light. It collides with the stone bridge in a **violent burst**, yet the water does not spill off the sides—rather, it **splits and streams off** in both directions, swirling along the edges of the bridge as if pulled by some invisible force.

The **black waterfall** continues to pulse, surging in rhythmic waves, its magic surrounding the bridge with an ominous hum, filling the air with a sense of **foreboding power**. The bridge, though strong, seems to be under pressure, as though it is being tested.

#### Cassius's Attitude
- Cassius initially **walks with purpose** at the start of the bridge but stops when it becomes clear that the trial is to be faced alone.
- He **watches intently**, gauging how the party responds to the growing danger.
- He does not try to prevent the collapse or shield them in any direct way. Instead, he remains an observer, understanding that the **trial of the bridge** is meant to test their **resourcefulness** and **courage**.
- As the **magical barrier** from the waterfall intensifies, Cassius’s hands remain **folded behind his back**, though his eyes gleam with a quiet intensity as he **studies their actions**. His demeanor is calm, perhaps almost detached from the immediate threat around them.

---
### **How the Puzzle Unfolds**

#### **1. The Waterfall’s Challenge**

As the players approach the center of the bridge, they realize that the **waterfall’s flow** is not simply an environmental feature—it is a **magical barrier**. The water exudes an aura of **darkness and ancient magic**, preventing passage beyond the middle of the bridge.

When the players first approach the **Dark Waterfall**, they quickly realize that the water is enchanted and cannot be simply passed through. Here, the **Arcana**, **Nature**, and **Investigation** checks come into play.

> [!Arcana Check]- Arcana Check (DC 14)
> A character trained in **Arcana** can sense the **magical aura** emanating from the waterfall. They might recognize the enchantment as one that **manipulates shadow magic** or is tied to the ancient wards protecting the Hall. 
> **Success:** The character gains insight into the fact that the water is a **magical barrier** and not a physical one, which can be altered by magic. They might deduce that casting **light-based spells** or using **radiant magic** could weaken it. The player might also recognize the need for a specific type of magic to break the enchantment. 
> **Failure:** They don’t grasp the true nature of the water but may still gain a general sense of danger around it.

>[!Nature Check]- **Nature Check (DC 12)**  
>A character with knowledge of **nature** may notice the odd, unnatural flow of the water or how it seems to resist the natural laws of fluid dynamics. They may recognize that this is not ordinary water, but a **magical manifestation**.  
>**Success:** They deduce that the water’s unnatural behavior is being controlled by the environment around it. They may suggest using **earth magic** or even **druidic magic** to interact with the water or the elements surrounding it.  
>**Failure:** They are unsure how the water works and may think it's simply an obstacle that must be bypassed with brute force.

> [!Investigation Check]- **Investigation Check (DC 13)** 
> A character who closely examines the **bridge** might notice **faint symbols** etched into the stone beneath the waterfall. These symbols pulse lightly, hinting at **activation triggers** tied to the waterfall’s magic. 
> **Success:** The character identifies the **ancient runes**, realizing they are the key to opening the path. They may also detect areas along the bridge where **magical focus** could alter the waterfall’s behavior.
> **Failure:** They are unable to interpret the runes, making it harder to discern what needs to be done.
#### **2. The Waterfall's True Nature**

- The waterfall is **magically charged** and can be interacted with in several ways:
    - **Magical Analysis:** A character skilled in arcane arts might **sense a lingering enchantment** within the waterfall, realizing it is not solid but a manifestation of ancient magic that can be manipulated.
    - **Testing the Water:** One character might be brave (or foolish) enough to try touching the water. As they do, the **black liquid** feels unnervingly cold, its surface darkening under their touch. The waterfall reacts by **splitting and reshaping**, forming more intricate tendrils or barriers.
    - **Environmental Interaction:** The players may begin to notice **symbols etched into the bridge's surface**, hidden beneath the layers of ancient stone, offering clues for how to overcome the obstacle. They might even sense **faint, magical resonances** in the air around them that seem to react to specific words or movements.

##### Touching the Waterfall
- **Magical Reaction:** The waterfall responds aggressively to any touch. The cascade of magical energy intensifies for a moment, **bursting outward** in a ripple of shadowy mist that briefly fills the area.
    - All characters nearby must make a **Dexterity Saving Throw (DC 14)** to avoid being **pushed back** by the magical force.
        - **Success:** The character resists the push but might still be knocked off balance.
        - **Failure:** The character is **pushed backwards**, receiving 1d4 points of damage and the cascade continues for a moment, intensifying the danger of the environment.
- **The Hall’s Response:** The Hall itself seems to **react to the touch**—a low hum reverberates from the waterfall, indicating that it is now **alerted to the intruders**.
#### **3. The Puzzle Element: How to Cross**

The puzzle here is a **test of unity and understanding**—the players must **manipulate the waterfall** in a way that allows them to pass through. They must **engage with the magical forces** that control the water. The solution lies in working together, as the waterfall’s magic only responds to those who are united in their efforts.

- **Key Elements:**
    - **The Elements of Nature:** The players may need to work with the natural elements around them—perhaps **fire or light magic** can dissipate the dark energy of the water, or **earth magic** can anchor their steps across the bridge.
    - **Personal Will:** The players’ own **strength of will** is critical here. Those who **focus their intent on the collective goal** (crossing together), or those who **perform a ritual** to shift the magic, may find the waterfall’s resistance weakening.

---

## **Cliffhanger: The Bridge Shifts**

Once the players solve the puzzle and **dispel the waterfall’s magic**, the **bridge begins to shift**, **cracking and groaning** as the stones realign. Suddenly, the **dark water’s flow reverses**, spiraling upward as if obeying an unseen force. The passage forward becomes clear—but only for a moment.

Just as they begin to move forward, **a new challenge appears**—the very **stone bridge** beneath their feet begins to **shift and crack**, as if the entire structure of the Hall is testing them. The players must **cross quickly** as the bridge begins to collapse or fall to the abyss.

### **Cassius’s Actions During the Collapse of the Bridge**

As the bridge begins to crumble under the intense magical pressure from the waterfall, **Professor Cassius Faulkner** stands at a distance, watching the players carefully but offering little direct intervention. His role in the scene is one of **guidance**, but not direct aid. He is a mentor, not a rescuer, and he respects the importance of **self-reliance** during the trial.
#### **During the Collapse:**

As the **bridge starts to fall apart**, Cassius’s role is still one of **indirect aid**:

- If a **player is in immediate danger of falling**, Cassius might raise a hand or subtly direct a **glimmer of light** from his wand to illuminate their path, but this is more **symbolic** than practical assistance.
- He may also **mutter something under his breath**, as if unlocking a **small protective charm**—enough to give the party a fighting chance, but not to fully interfere.

> _“The Hall only welcomes those who face its trials... head-on.”_

He does not interfere in the **final moments of collapse**. Instead, he is prepared to **assist after** they’ve made it across. This is part of his philosophy—he trusts the players to **earn their place** through their **own actions**.

---
#### **After the Collapse:**

Once they reach the other side, the **magical hum** of the Hall greets them. Only then does Cassius finally speak at the other side of the now collapsed bridge, his voice low but satisfied:

> _“You have proven yourselves worthy of this place. The rest is up to you.”_  
> _“I will stay behind to keep the doorway open.”_  
> _“Now... find what you are meant to discover.”_

In this moment, Cassius reveals his role as more of a **guide** than a companion, leaving them to face the **Hall's dangers** on their own while ensuring they are not locked out of the trials they are meant to face.

He does not follow them into the Hall; instead, he watches them from the entrance, his **wand glowing faintly** as he maintains the magical door's opening, ensuring that they can return should they need to.
